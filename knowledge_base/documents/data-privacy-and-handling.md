# Data Privacy and Handling

> SYNTHETIC POLICY. Invented for the FIN-GUARD research prototype. It is not real regulation or legal advice.

## Personal data in logs and reports
Logs and case reports must contain identifiers, not full personal details. Log the customer identifier and the last four characters of a document number instead of names, dates of birth or full document numbers. Display full personal details only to reviewers who need them for the case.

## Access control
Access is role based. Analysts can view and decide cases, auditors can view the audit trail but not change decisions, and administrators manage configuration but cannot decide cases. Every access to a customer record is recorded.

## Synthetic data notice
This prototype uses only synthetic data. No real personal data may be placed in the repository, the test data or the knowledge base. Results measured on synthetic data must never be described as the performance on real customers.

## On-premise processing
Customer data and model inference stay inside the institution's own infrastructure. No customer data may be sent to an external commercial service.
