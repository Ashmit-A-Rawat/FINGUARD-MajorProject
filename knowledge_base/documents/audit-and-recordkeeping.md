# Audit Trail and Record Keeping

> SYNTHETIC POLICY. Invented for the FIN-GUARD research prototype. It is not real regulation or legal advice. Retention periods are illustrative.

## What is logged
Every case keeps an audit trail of who did what and when. The trail records the case identifier, the request identifier, the agent or reviewer, the model name and version, the prompt version, the documents retrieved, the latency, any errors, the decision and the human reviewer. Sensitive personal data is not copied into logs unless strictly necessary.

## Retention
Audit records are kept for a minimum of five years in this policy and may not be edited or deleted during that time. Corrections are made by adding a new entry that refers to the original.

## Reproducibility
It must be possible to reproduce how a case reached its outcome. Store the versions of the models, prompts, rules and knowledge-base documents used, so that a reviewer can later see exactly what information the system had.
