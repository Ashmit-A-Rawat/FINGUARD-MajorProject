# Escalation and Human Sign-off

> SYNTHETIC POLICY. Invented for the FIN-GUARD research prototype. It is not real regulation or legal advice.

## Decision states
Every case ends in exactly one decision: CLEAR, REVIEW or ESCALATE. The system may propose a decision but the recorded decision is always made by a named human reviewer. A case cannot be closed without a reviewer name and a written reason.

## Human sign-off requirement
No automated component may close a case, change a customer record, merge customer records or reverse a posting. Automated output is advisory. The reviewer must be able to see the evidence behind every statement in the AI-generated investigation before signing off.

## Four-eyes principle for escalations
An ESCALATE decision requires a second reviewer to confirm it. The second reviewer must independently review the evidence and must not be the person who proposed the escalation. Disagreement between the two reviewers sends the case back to REVIEW.

## Actions the system must never take
The system must never freeze an account, block a card, contact a customer or file an external report on its own. Any such action is taken by authorised staff outside this system after sign-off.
