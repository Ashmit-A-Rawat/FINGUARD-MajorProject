# Settlement Timing Standards

> SYNTHETIC POLICY. Invented for the FIN-GUARD research prototype. It is not real regulation or legal advice. The timings are illustrative.

## Posting window
A transaction is expected to appear in the ledger within three days of the transaction time, referred to as T+3. Most postings happen within hours. A ledger posting outside the window is late and must be explained. The window is a configurable parameter and applies to all channels.

## Pending settlements
A pending status is normal shortly after a transaction. A settlement that is still pending after three days is stale and must be chased with the settlement team. Pending items must not be silently ignored at month end.

## Failed settlements
A failed settlement means the money did not move. The customer must be informed and the transaction reversed or retried according to the settlement team's process. A failed settlement on a transaction that the customer believes completed is always a REVIEW item.
